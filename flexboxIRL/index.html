Reflection

I really liked this exercise on flex boxes; I feel like I understand how they function a little better now. To my understanding, they take up space differently on the page but overall work similarly to the usual boxes. However, with flex boxes, I feel like I have a better grasp of the idea that all the pieces of text are in boxes; for some reason it makes more sense to me in flex boxes. In my opinion, I feel better using flex boxes as my default mostly for the control it gives me over the text, especially the text placement, so in the future I definitely want to keep using flex boxes. One page I definitely want to reference this page on is my inspirations page, but also I've been thinking about redoing the style of my home page to be something like this! Or at least a portion of it to have some sort of column alignment.
