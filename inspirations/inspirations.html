Aryn's Inspirations

Bluffy Cream
@bluffycr3am

Her art is what I find myself often going back to referencing when it comes to anatomy and art style, especially in the way she draws faces and poses. Despite the language barrier (her posts are in Spanish), I am still inspired by her dynamic poses and facial expressions with clean lines, clear shapes, bright colors, and a confidence in her highlights and shadows that I really admire. The way she draws faces and bodies has a beautiful balance of realistic to cartoonish proportions that I like to study and try to emulate in my own art. We're also the same age.

Omo.core

@omo.core

His art is just straight up beautiful. I love digital paintings because it's so different from my style and process that I really admire those who do it. However, what drew me to his art are his colors, especially the ones depicting shadows. Although most of the characters he draws are very simple, his style shows such a gorgeous understanding of lighting that it adds such depth to a little mug or a literal Roblox character. The shadows are often vibrant blues and greens that accentuate the often plain white characters. I love how colorful shadows look and it often makes me look forward to the coloring stage (a stage I don't usually like at all). His animations are also incredibly smooth; it's hard for me to believe this is a 16 year old.

Anna Laura Art

@annalauraart

Although I do admire her watercolors and her lovely backgrounds, I most connect with her messaging in all her art. Her warm colors and animal characters are used to tell sweet stories that make whoever views them feel a little more grateful for being alive—a message I find really valuable and I take a lot of inspiration from for my day to day life.

Reflection

For this page, I wanted to focus on making my code look neater in HTML. I tried spacing things I thought would make sense and be consistent to make looking at specific pieces of code easier. For example, I put the "[a]" tag over the artist's name instead of the link. The pattern I made for each paragraph made it easier to spot what I did wrong and then copy/paste the tag into the right spot. However, the [a] tag doesn't seem to be working when I load the site, so my links aren't quite working. In the future I would like to look more into that when I know more commands, for lack of better term. Among the three artists I chose, I noticed that I really like expressive characters with vibrant colors. I liked noticing my pattern in colors because it contrasts with the colors I'm usually drawn to in my life. In my clothes or my room, I usually gravitate towards earthier colors like browns and creams. However, in my art, places like my sketchbook and my laptop, I tend to really like brighter and dynamic colors.
