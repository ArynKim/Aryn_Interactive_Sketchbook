About Me


Hi, my name is Aryn and the clip above is just a timelapse of me doing homework! I like to take timelapses of me doing work to keep me off my phone and to show myself in the future that I can do what I need to do if I ever need a little reminder sometimes. I'm a 2nd year at San Francisco State University and a Graphic Design Major (aka Visual Communications Design) and Tech Theatre Minor. A current project I have right now is a combination of both: designing the promotional posters for my high school's shows for this season. I'm from San Mateo so I am still very much in touch with my drama teacher/director, as well as their program as a whole.

When I'm not doing something design or theatre related, I also skateboard and work at a skate shop at the mall! I love to cruise around the city, something I do a lot more now that I live in the city and commute to campus and to work. It's always been a dream to live in SF because it has always felt like home to me—I often tell people I don't think I could leave it. Reflection

For my video portrait, I decided to use a timelapse I already had on my phone. I honestly have years of timelapses of me just doing some kind of work and I thought the one I used in this page gives a pretty decent idea of me. I would have liked one where I'm doing something a little more interesting, but I liked this one because it was taken the most recently and would therefore give just a more updated idea of myself.

Before this assignment, I had no idea that Youtube can provide a set of code you can copy/paste into a website; it's a feature I would have never considered or even thought I would have needed ever. It makes me wonder what other sites include something like this and how often it is used. Unfortunately, I can't quite get the video to load correctly on my site. I first tried using the stripped-down example in the assignment description, but after the thumbnail wouldn't load I decided to try copy/pasting exactly what Youtube provided, however that lead to the same result. In the future, I would like to better understand what is going on in the code provided and hopefully that will lead me to being able to solve my issue of not having the video directly on the site.
